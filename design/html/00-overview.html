SKILL PORTAL · REDESIGN OVERVIEW

Help people grow. One loop. Five places.

Every screen serves one step of the loop below. Employees see 5 places built around improving themselves; managers add 3 to understand and help their team (reviewing lives in Team); admins get a separate console. Nothing on screen claims more than the data supports.
